- 360 / 2 = 180 (half-circle)
- 360 / 3 = 120 (trigrams × 3)
- 360 / 4 = 90 (quadrants)
- 360 / 5 = 72 (pentagrams, Wu Xing)
- 360 / 6 = 60 (sexagesimal)
- 360 / 8 = 45 (octants, trigrams)
