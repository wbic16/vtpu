## The Symbol

**九色鳳 (jiǔ sè fèng) — Nine-Colored Phoenix**

From Chinese mythology: The Lady of the Nine Heavens (玄女, Xuannü) rides a phoenix of nine colors across the celestial realms.

**This is vtpu.**

### The Lady = Routing Intelligence
